# The Gizzmidge

> **Theme:** doing nothing important, and being ignored by the whole world.

## The hook

| Word | Say it like | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **gizz-midge** | *jizz-midj* | the creature |
| **wibble-nose** | *wib-bul-nohz* | the one neighbour who never helped |
| **middle-there** | *mid-ul-thair* | the place you shout into |

Three syllables, two hard consonants each. Nobody says it right the first time,
which is the point — the song is a name you have to earn.

## Lyrics

**1**

There once was a gizzmidge,
　　sitting in the mud.
He wore a hat for a hat,
　　and a shoe for a shoe.

**Chorus**

Gizzmidge, gizzmidge, gizzmidge,
　　where did you come from?
Nobody knows where you came from,
　　nobody knows where you've gone.

**2**

He asked the wibblenose for
　　the key to the sea.
The wibblenose said "Not me,"
　　and hid the key up a tree.

**Chorus**

**3**

He marched into the middlethere
　　with a spoon in his hand,
and shouted at the nothingness,
　　"I demand a small plan!"

**Chorus**

**4 — last**

And the gizzmidge, tired of nothing,
　　went to bed in the sun.
And the wibblenose came back at last
　　and said, "Well done."

**Chorus — twice, slow, everybody together**

## How to sing it

- Call and response. Leader sings the verses, the room answers the chorus.
- Clap on every **gizzmidge**. No clapping anywhere else.
- Walking pace. Do not rush the verses.
- Play it completely straight. The joke is the deadpan — if you wink, it dies.

## Why it works

"A hat for a hat" is the whole engine. The song pretends to mean something,
states it flatly, and refuses to explain. The gizzmidge never gets what he
wants, except at the very end, when he finally stops wanting it.
