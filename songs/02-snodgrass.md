# Snodgrass

> **Theme:** being so stubborn that refusing becomes the entire personality.

## The hook

| Word | Say it like | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **snodgrass** | *snod-grass* | rhymes with *grass*, so it lands on the first try |

This is the friendly one. No hard consonants, no invented mouth-feel — just a
stupid word stuck to an ordinary one. Children say it correctly at once, which
means they can join in on the first repeat.

## Lyrics

**1**

Once there was a snodgrass,
　　standing in the rain.
It said, "I will not move,"
　　and it did not move again.

**Chorus**

Snodgrass, snodgrass, standing in the rain,
　　everybody's asking, "Are you all right?"
Snodgrass never said a word. It's still there.

**2**

A sparrow came and asked it,
　　"Sir, is this your home?"
The snodgrass said, "Perhaps,"
　　and stood there on its own.

**Chorus**

**3**

The wind came up and asked it,
　　"May I lean on you?"
The snodgrass said, "Not now,"
　　and the wind withdrew.

**Chorus**

**4 — last**

The rain gave up in winter,
　　the sun came out in May.
The snodgrass is still standing there.
　　It's snodgrass. That's its way.

**Chorus — once, then stop dead**

## How to sing it

- Call and response. Adults sing the verses, children shout the chorus.
- On the last chorus, stop after "never said a word" and just stand there.
  Silence is the punchline.
- Snug and slow. This one wants patience, not energy.

## Why it works

Every verse is a different visitor being politely refused. The joke is the
refusal never changes and never explains itself. "It's snodgrass. That's its
way." is the most honest ending in this repo.
