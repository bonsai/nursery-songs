I bought a fruit at the shop on a Friday,
　　and the fruit said, "I am not this."
I said, "You are a pomelo, I would think it is,"
　　and the fruit said, "Ask me again, or miss."

( chorus )
What's your name? — Pomelo.
What's your name? — Pomelo.
What's your name? — Pomelo.
Oh, you silly pomelo.

I said, "Are you a lemon? A lime? A grapefruit?"
　　and the fruit said, "I am not like that."
I said, "You are a pomelo, you are big and pale."
　　and the fruit said, "I was never that stale."

( chorus )

I said, "Are you a persimmon? Are you a pear?"
　　and the fruit said, "Do not come near."
I said, "You are a pomelo, that is what you are."
　　and the fruit said, "I have never been a pear."

( chorus )

I opened my hand — and the fruit was a shoe,
　　then the shop said, "Pomelo? Not for you."

( chorus )
What's your name? — Pomelo.
What's your name? — Pomelo.
What's your name? — Pomelo.
Oh, you silly pomelo. Oh, you silly pomelo.
