# Wibbledoo

> **Theme:** refusing to fall asleep, and the strange company that keeps you up.

## The hook

| Word | Say it like | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **wibbledoo** | *wib-bul-DOO* | lilted, three syllables, meant to be sung in one breath |

The only hook in this repo with a tune built into it. The *doo* carries the
note. Children get the lilt right faster than they get the consonants.

## Lyrics

**1**

Wibbledoo went to bed,
　　wibbledoo shut his eye.
Wibbledoo said, "I'm not asleep yet,"
　　and neither was I.

**Chorus**

Wibbledoo, wibbledoo, nobody's asleep,
　　the moon is doing overtime again.
Wibbledoo, wibbledoo, one more minute,
　　everybody does it. Everybody. Everyone.

**2**

Wibbledoo counted a sheep,
　　but the sheep counted him.
Wibbledoo counted a second sheep,
　　and the second sheep did the same.

**Chorus**

**3**

Wibbledoo said to the dark,
　　"Are you finished with me?"
The dark said, "I am always finished.
　　I am the dark. Can't you see?"

**Chorus**

**4 — last**

So wibbledoo lay quite still,
　　and he did not move.
And at last he was asleep,
　　and he dreamed of a dove.

And the dove said, "Sleep now."
　　And the dark said, "Good night."
And wibbledoo slept like a pebble,
　　which is heavy and white.

**Chorus — slow, and then a long pause before the last line**

## How to sing it

- This is a bedtime song. Lullaby tempo, no percussion, no clapping.
- Say the last line of the chorus three times, each one slower:
  *everybody does it. everybody. everyone.*
- The dove section is spoken, not sung, if you can manage it.
- Stop singing the moment you finish. Do not add a "goodnight."

## Why it works

The children already know this song by the time they finish verse one. Every
object in it — the sheep, the dark, the moon doing overtime — is something they
have actually thought about at 2am. The joke is that the sheep counts him
back.
